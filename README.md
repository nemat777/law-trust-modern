# Gregory Law Redesign

Build a modern redesign of the Gregory Law Offices website (https://www.gregorylawoffices.com/):

- Professional, high-trust law firm design with a modern navy/slate and warm neutral aesthetic, elegant typography, and mobile-responsive layout
- Authoritative hero section highlighting trusted counsel for Illinois businesses, real estate, and families with immediate CTAs to schedule a consultation or call
- Segmented practice areas covering Business Transactions & Corporate Formation, Real Estate (Commercial & Residential), Estate Planning (Wills, Trusts, Powers of Attorney), Probate Administration, and Civil Litigation
- Attorney profile for Tom P. Gregory highlighting 21+ years of experience, John Marshall Law School credentials, and representation across Circuit, Appellate, and Illinois Supreme Court levels in Park Ridge, IL
- Interactive client consultation intake form with practice area selection and clear contact details
- Client utilities section including direct phone, office hours, location map/details, and secure client payment portal link
